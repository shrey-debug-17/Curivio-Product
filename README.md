# Curivio-Product

Policy-bounded AI training for companies. Curivio teaches employees how to use AI at work, using only your organisation's approved sources. It never teaches anything your own policies prohibit. And it produces audit-ready evidence of who learned what, when, and from which sources.

Company policy guardrails. Upload your AI / data-handling policies. Curivio extracts rules for an admin to approve, removes any lesson content that conflicts with them, and teaches the policy in a "What your company allows" section.
Grounded lessons. A 7-agent pipeline builds each lesson from your Source Registry. Every sentence is cited to a graded source (company policy › regulation › SOP › vetted reference › open web).
Personalised. A diagnostic sets each learner's depth. Failing a check brings the module back as a gentler review. Lessons can be tailored per job role when a model is connected.
Evidence. A hash-chained, tamper-evident audit log, plus PDF/CSV evidence exports for auditors and boards.
Learning kits. Every built-in module ships diagrams (flowcharts, cycles, concept maps), tables, before/after examples, do/don't cards, practice scenarios and hand-verified YouTube videos (embedded, with an "Open on YouTube" link).
Adaptive checks. 3–9 questions per lesson, sized by the learner's level and how much the lesson teaches; some questions come from the diagrams and tables.
Explainable AI tutor. Learners ask questions on any lesson. Answers come only from approved sources, are tailored to the learner's role, department and level, pass the policy guard, and show why: persona used, sources with authority and match strength, policy rules checked, confidence, and the steps taken. Off-topic questions get an honest "not in your sources".
Polished, responsive UI. Full-width landing page that scales from 320px phones to 2560px monitors; light/dark theme switch (remembered per browser, with an animated reveal); a trailing cursor ring, magnetic and "shine" buttons, spotlight cards and 3D tilt on product shots (mouse only, off for touch screens and "reduce motion"); scroll progress, section-aware navigation and a back-to-top button.
Command palette. Press Ctrl+K (or /) anywhere in the app to jump to any page, track or lesson, switch theme or sign out.
Lessons in English, हिन्दी or ગુજરાતી. English is the default and the official version; Hindi and Gujarati translations are made by the connected model and cached. Grading always uses the English lesson.
